# Asset acknowledgments

## Earth texture

`HW2/earth_feb.jpg` and `HW3/earth_feb.jpg` are identical copies of the
February 2004 **Blue Marble: Next Generation** global composite.

- Credit: Reto Stöckli, NASA Earth Observatory.
- Source: [NASA Visible Earth — February, Blue Marble Next Generation](https://www.visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/73967/february-blue-marble-next-generation/73978l).
- Data source: Terra/MODIS; acquired February 1–29, 2004.

NASA requests that republished Blue Marble: Next Generation material credit
“NASA Earth Observatory.” Its appearance here is factual and does not imply
NASA endorsement of this repository.

## Visualization models and data

`Viz/CubeSat-2U.glb`, `Viz/ICESat.glb`, `Viz/sat_data.mat`, and
`Viz/sat_rel_dyn_data.mat` are the exact model and saved-data files used by the
coursework visualization scripts. They are included so the retained scripts can
run from the `Viz/` directory without code changes.
